Reserved series notation stays distinct: M_cell=B^T B is the Cell3 Gram metric, R_cell=M_cell⁻¹ its inverse, G_phase is a separate phase metric, and O_K is the physical origin. None is a Yukawa matrix or a mass map in this course. No Cell3 numerical geometry, rounding convention, dyadic/ternary recurrence, or moving celestial frame is changed or used as an interaction premise.

Evidence vocabulary: a source report is what a located artifact states; a fresh derivation is an operation recomputed here; a synthetic example is deliberately supplied teaching data; a proposed observation is not acquired data; an unresolved coordinate is an input not yet supplied. Reference-theory vocabulary does not by itself confer fresh proof or measurement status.
