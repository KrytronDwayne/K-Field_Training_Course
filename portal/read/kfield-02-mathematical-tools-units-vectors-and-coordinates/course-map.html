Course map — Mathematical Tools: Units, Vectors, and Coordinates

This is course-02, a complete topical course for high-school seniors: seven 55-minute lessons and two 55-minute capstone periods, 495 minutes total. The entry capabilities are signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, integer powers, simple linear equations, and reading coordinate graphs. Course-01 supplies the habit of distinguishing inputs and evidence from derived claims; Lesson 01 restates this bridge so no inaccessible prior artifact is needed for core arithmetic.

The course uses synthetic 2D examples throughout. No prior trigonometry, university linear algebra, calculus, or physics is assumed. Lessons teach right-triangle ratios and vector normalization before projections, then substitution before a general inverse. Advanced source terminology is recognition-only. Units 1, 2, and 3 end after Lessons 02, 04, and 07 respectively.

The lesson order remains the approved seven-lesson map. Required bridge material was added within the existing boundaries rather than assumed: magnitude/Pythagorean explanation in L03; sine/cosine and non-unit projection in L04; determinant, singularity, and inverse derivation in L06. The teacher guide reserves most independent practice time in these denser lessons for their four diagnostics. Teachers may extend a lesson with remediation when necessary; no core concept is optional.

Deliverable route

Read lesson-01.md through lesson-07.md, then capstone.md. course.md assembles the seven lessons verbatim in that order and links the capstone and supports. Each standalone lesson includes worked answers; instructors can hide those sections during practice. terminology.md contains the actual used notation; teacher-guide.md provides pacing, feedback, and assessment guidance. sources.md and source-records.json keep compact provenance. verify_examples.py and worked-example-verification.json give reproducible synthetic arithmetic checks. audit_report.json records findings and verification; manifest.json and completion_report.json carry recovery statuses and hashes.

Evidence and recovery boundaries

Inputs are synthetic; derived values are exact mathematics unless explicitly displayed approximately. Source conventions remain source-local. There is no experimental interpretation, proposed mechanism, or empirical evidence claim to assess in this course. A retained perpendicular component and a retained rounding residual are different coordinates, each with its own reconstruction rule.
