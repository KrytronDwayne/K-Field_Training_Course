Course map — Complex Carriers and Gauge Symmetry

Seven lessons, twelve 55-minute core periods and three capstone periods. The approved lesson boundaries are retained. Multi-period lessons separate arithmetic, interpretation, and verification rather than adding hidden prerequisites. All examples are synthetic and dimensionless. No source data are copied.

Entry capabilities: signed arithmetic, fractions, powers, linear equations, coordinate plotting, and bracket expansion. Course-13 is the series predecessor; this course explicitly bridges complex numbers, matrices, determinants, groups and kernels.

Dependency chain: lesson-01 → lesson-02 → lesson-03 → lesson-04 → lesson-05 → lesson-06 → lesson-07 → capstone. Unit boundaries follow lessons 3 and 5.

Every concept is introduced, practiced with a worked answer, diagnosed, and reused. Legacy IDs C14-CX-EX1, C14-CX-P1 and C14-KER-D1 are preserved. SA-14 and EA-14-E06-2 are implemented in the named lessons. Each lesson has four diagnostics with worked answers. The capstone backward map names the prior practice for every assessed operation.

Core scope: exact small complex calculations, actual matrices, typed permutations, determinant products, finite kernels, local actions and source comparison. Recognition-only: full connected-group classification, topology of covers, general exterior-algebra proof, compiler execution, and physical global-form uniqueness. Source-local M10 requires M09 in the backbone; operational prerequisites are bridged here rather than asserting fresh proof of M09.

Required source fidelity: use color-first C₃⊕W₂ and weights (−2,−2,−2,3,3); retain weak-first displays only with P. Preserve the mature Z6 construction and integration family Z1,Z2,Z3,Z6 in separate records. Mathematical-backbone and compiler module namespaces remain distinct. Other supplied geometry/metric invariants remain reserved and are not imported into this topic without their separate contracts.
