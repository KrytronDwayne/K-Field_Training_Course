Teacher guide — Complex Carriers and Gauge Symmetry

Audience: high-school seniors with signed arithmetic, fractions, powers, linear equations, coordinate plots, and bracket expansion. Course-13 is the series prerequisite; all extra operations needed here are taught explicitly, so unavailable course-13 text is not a hidden assessment dependency. No calculus, university algebra, or quantum mechanics is assumed.

Use twelve core periods plus three capstone periods, each 55 minutes. In each core period allocate about 5 minutes to retrieval, 20 to explanation and a worked example, 15 to guided practice, and 15 to independent diagnostics and correction. Multi-period lessons repeat that structure with the subdivisions below. Keep the written answers covered until students have attempted the task. Students retain their initial work and correction alongside it.

Lesson Periods Teaching sequence and diagnostic response
1 2 First expand complex products and conjugates. Then use coordinate quarter-turns and undo them. If a student calls −i the imaginary component of 2−i, return to (2,−1).
2 2 First teach row-column multiplication and dagger. Then work through V entry by entry. If transpose is substituted for dagger, compare VᵀV and V†V explicitly.
3 1 Mark five physical paper slots c₁,c₂,c₃,w₁,w₂. Reorder weak-first cards using P; move the charge entries too. Retain both projections.
4 2 First teach ad−bc and diagonal products. Then the top-form rule and exponent 3a+2b. Contrast diag(2,1/2) with diag(i,1); neither meets both tests.
5 3 First enumerate mod-6 maps. Second solve Φ's identity equations. Third complete six rows and prove exhaustion. Do not replace the source kernel with the toy analogy.
6 1 Supply group, carrier and action in separate fields. Compare global and local phases, and ρ₁ with ρ₂. Ask what dynamics remain unspecified.
7 1 Give source cards. Have pairs reconstruct two rows without collapsing either status. Grade exact scope and namespace, not deference to a title.

Unit boundaries after lessons 3 and 5: ask students to reconstruct a tuple from retained components; audit symbol meanings and prerequisites before introducing determinants or actions. After lesson 7, walk backward through every capstone item and locate its answered practice. Full group classification, general cover topology, exterior-algebra construction, anomaly proofs, and compiler execution are recognition-only or future work. Do not assess them here.

Teacher verification: lessons supply four answered diagnostics each. Independently recompute at least one new input before instruction, using the same taught operations. The provided verify_examples.py checks finite matrix identities, inverse recovery, all source-kernel exponents, permutations, and key negative controls using standard-library arithmetic. It is verification support, not a student coding prerequisite. verification.json records the actual run; audit_report.json records the review and repairs.

Evidence discipline: distinguish source records, our finite deductions, interpretations, proposed mechanisms, reference definitions and unresolved questions. S02 M10 uses a weak-first display; S03 corrects storage to color-first. The full 5×5 permutation must accompany any order change. Integer phase weights are dimensionless; no geometric metric or physical charge scale is substituted. The mature determinant-lock construction and the integration's Z1,Z2,Z3,Z6 family answer different scoped questions. Compiler M03,M07,M08 are not backbone labels. Source proof-status strings and certificates are not fresh typechecking here.

Information accounting: a unitary map is reversed using its retained dagger; a permutation using Pᵀ; projections using their retained pair. A quotient requires a representative and kernel label. A norm cannot replace full amplitudes. No observations are filtered or erased in this mathematical course. Preserve student disagreements as specific unresolved learning questions and preserve all source branches in the comparison.

Capstone administration: use the three periods described in capstone.md. The rubric totals 100 points and all seven operations have prior answered practice. Accept equivalent exact algebra and properly declared notation. Diagnose a high score with an order or scope error by the specific defect; do not let a total hide an unresolved core misconception. Additional practice can change the small input numbers while keeping the same operations.
