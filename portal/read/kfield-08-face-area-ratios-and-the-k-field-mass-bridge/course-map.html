Course map — Face-Area Ratios and the K-Field Mass Bridge

Six 55-minute lessons followed by two 55-minute capstone periods. Unit I (1–3): ratios and evaluation. Unit II (4–6): provenance, interpretation, and finite sensitivity. No boundary changes were required. Entry capabilities: signed arithmetic, fractions, powers, linear equations, and labeled face areas from courses 03 and 07; explicit bridges appear in lessons 1–3.

Dependency chain: lesson-01 → lesson-02 → lesson-03 → lesson-04 → lesson-05 → lesson-06 → capstone. Lesson 3 also teaches E2, the locked source-reading card used by lessons 4 and the capstone.

Each lesson allocates 5 minutes retrieval, 20 instruction/worked example, 15 answered guided practice, and 15 independent diagnostics/repair. Long source fractions are supplied reading cards, not manual long-division tasks. If retrieval fails, use the teacher-guide repair task before moving to a dependent lesson.

The capstone backward map names inputs, outputs, and earlier answered practice in capstone.md. Evidence boundaries and every packet invariant remain authoritative; only relevant finite operations are assessed.
