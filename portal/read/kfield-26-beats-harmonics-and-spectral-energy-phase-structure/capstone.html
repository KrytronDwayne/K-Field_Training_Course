Capstone — A complete signal and evidence packet

Allow three 55-minute periods. Session 1 completes the input ledger and steps 1–3; session 2 completes steps 4–6; session 3 completes steps 7–9, checks reconstruction and repairs errors. All required operations were practiced; no external source access is needed to answer the source-card questions. Use the supplied source facts with their stated evidence class.

All numeric signals below are synthetic except the explicitly labeled archive card in step 7. Keep counts, time units, angle conventions, reference phases and signs. Use the four-point DFT forward sign minus and inverse sign plus with factor 1/4. Unless otherwise stated Δt=1/4 s, time origin zero, angles in radians.

Student tasks

  1. C26-CAP-01 (4 points; C26.01-E1/W1/P1/D1). For y(t)=3 cos(2πt) counts, state f,T,ω, baseline and phase. Tabulate samples at 0,1/4,1/2,3/4 s and the samples after adding π to phase. State the metadata needed to reconstruct them.
  2. C26-CAP-02 (4 points; C26.02-E1/W1/P1/D1, legacy C26-BEAT-P1). Analyze s(t)=cos(2π·5t)+cos(2π·7t) counts. Derive its product form from the taught addition rules. Give carrier frequency, signed-factor frequency and absolute-envelope peak rate. Calculate t=1/2 s and explain what an unsigned replacement would lose.
  3. C26-CAP-03 (4 points; C26.03-E1/W1/P1/D1). Take n=(1,0,0), O=x(0)=(0,0,0) m, v=(−1,0,0) m/s, d=1/2 m. Find the signed fundamental rate and k=2 harmonic. Evaluate its cosine/sine pair at 1/16 s. Explain why the cosine alone cannot distinguish this motion from the reversed motion.
  4. C26-CAP-04 (4 points; C26.04-E1/W1/P1/D1, legacy C26-DFT-P1). Transform and reconstruct both (1,1,1,1) and (1,2,3,4) counts. Give all four complex coefficients and all four inverse samples. Delay the latter one sample periodically and calculate its coefficients. Keep DC and Nyquist as well as conjugate bins.
  5. C26-CAP-05 (4 points; C26.05-E1/W1/P1/D1). For counts (2,5,4,3) at α,α+90°,α+180°,α+270°, calculate M,Q,D₀,D₉₀, contrast_counts and g; reconstruct the counts. Use synthetic |v·n|=3,c=10 in common speed units, sin θ=3/5, to calculate ρ,P. For coefficients R=2,Z=−2i, find the complex ratio and the ratio after reversing Z's polarity. State what to retain if R=0.
  6. C26-CAP-06 (4 points; C26.06-E1/W1/P1/D1). Use the previous g, toy power interval [3,6] energy-units/time-unit, exposure 2 time-units, h=6 energy-units·time-unit, c=10 length-units/time-unit, λ=3 length-units. Calculate ordered signed energy and photon-equivalent intervals. Explain the distinction from detected photons, then show why scalar output 6 cannot identify factors (2,3) versus (1,6).
  7. C26-CAP-07 (4 points; C26.07-E1/W1/P1/D1). Read this archive card: spectral /events/0, ID 415-515:415:w, has g=0.0005055808785109474, ρ=0.0000574509228616101 and null energy. Linked energy /all_event_channel_modulations/0 gives counts (1485.2562384582884,1483.8843576874326,1483.1281192291442,1483). Compute P and the two means/half-differences. State the reconstruction rule and retain numerical closure to your available precision. The complete ledger has w4,v4,lambda6 rows; their costs are 1.777875828850227,5.949871981229392,5.7964545517567245, respectively. Count levels and responses. Compare the evidence class with your synthetic beat calculation. Explain the significance of eight positive gains, six negative gains and five selected energy rows, and identify the recorded provenance gaps.
  8. C26-CAP-08 (4 points; C26.08-E1/W1/P1/D1). Decompose x=(3,1,−1,1) counts into even represented part and full odd residual. Transform and reconstruct both. Specify a next cross-channel comparison retaining the residual. Append these domain failures: A counts (2,0,4,0) with step-5 motion; B counts (8,4,6,2) with v·n=0,c=10,sin θ=3/5; C counts (8,4,6,2) with |v·n|=3,c=10,sin θ=0. Give defined intermediates and reasons for undefined results.
  9. C26-CAP-09 (4 points; C26.S01-E1/W1/P1/D1). For the same x, define H,E,O and prove E x+O x=x. Explain why a half-turn multiplies mode k by (−1)^k and why an even comparator cannot see odd modes. Distinguish the native ternary ladder, proposed Fibonacci cutoff ladder and proposed 25/27 difference pathway. Explain why a four-point transform does not resolve native high harmonics.

Full worked answer key

  1. f=1 Hz, T=1 s, ω=2π radians/s, b=0 counts, φ=0. Angles 0,π/2,π,3π/2 give (3,0,−3,0); adding π gives (−3,0,3,0). Retain origin, Δt=1/4 s, order, units, equation and phase shift.
  2. Add cos(a+b) and cos(a−b) to get 2 cos a cos b; put a=(u+v)/2,b=(u−v)/2. With u=10πt,v=14πt, s=2 cos(2πt)cos(12πt). Rates are 6,1 and 2 Hz. At 1/2 s s=−2 counts, whereas the unsigned-factor replacement gives +2. The signed factor carries polarity.
  3. qdot=(−1)/(1/2)=−2 cycles/s, q=−2t. k=2 gives phase −8πt and signed rate −4 cycles/s. At 1/16 s the pair is (0,−1). Cosine is even under phase reversal, but sine changes sign; preserve the normal orientation and origin.
  4. Constant input yields (4,0,0,0), inverse all ones. The other DFT is (10,−2+2i,−2,−2−2i). Inverse numerators are (4,8,12,16), yielding (1,2,3,4). Delay to (4,1,2,3), multiplying coefficients by (1,−i,−1,i), gives (10,2+2i,2,2−2i). Its inverse numerators (16,4,8,12) give that delayed sequence. Both spectra retain order and conjugacy.
  5. M=3,Q=4,D₀=−1,D₉₀=1 counts; contrast=−1 count; g=−1/4. Reconstruction (M+D₀,Q+D₉₀,M−D₀,Q−D₉₀) gives (2,5,4,3). ρ=3/(10·3/5)=1/2; P=−1/2. Z/R=−i, reversed ratio i. At R=0 retain R,Z and the undefined status, together with common sampling, polarity and wavelength metadata.
  6. Products are −3/2 and −3 energy-units; order them as [−3,−3/2]. Toy photon energy hc/λ=20 energy-units, so signed photon equivalent is [−3/20,−3/40]. This signed synthetic extension is not a negative detected-photon count and does not fill null source fields. Both 2·3 and 1·6 equal 6. Calibration/transfer evidence is needed to identify factors; an energy equivalent alone cannot select them.
  7. P≈8.800222056115778; M≈1484.1921788437162, Q≈1483.4421788437162, D₀≈1.064059614572102, D₉₀≈0.4421788437163059 counts. Contrast=0.75 count and g=0.75/Q. Use M±D₀ and Q±D₉₀ to reconstruct. The supplied binary64 replay retains input-minus-reconstruction (0,2.2737367544323206e−13,2.2737367544323206e−13,0) counts; a hand answer may state rounding and retain its own signed differences rather than assert exact computer closure. Three cost levels serve 14 responses. Eight positive and six negative gains are all retained; only five positive rows have source energy estimates, and null is not zero. The event remains reported_exploratory_to_be_verified, whereas the beat equality is a mathematical deduction from synthetic inputs. The two works' three representations are not six independent studies. The bundle records the energy revision mismatch and quadratic PDF digest/size mismatch; raw/calibration/uncertainty/exposure-transfer and independent-replay obligations remain.
  8. Hx=(−1,1,3,1), m=(1,1,1,1), r=(2,0,−2,0). Spectra are (4,0,0,0) and (0,4,0,4); inverse gives m and r, adding to x. Compare odd coefficients with a declared nonzero reference at the same times and bins; retain both m and r and any undefined ratio. Failure A: M=3,Q=0,D₀=−1,D₉₀=0,contrast=3,ρ=1/2; g,P undefined. B: M=7,Q=3,D₀=D₉₀=1,contrast=4,g=4/3,ρ=0; P undefined. C: same count coordinates and g as B; ρ and P undefined because sin θ=0. Keep all four counts for each row.
  9. H shifts by two entries modulo four, E=(I+H)/2, O=(I−H)/2; I means leave the sequence unchanged. Addition gives E+O=I. The half-turn factor exp(ikπ)=(−1)^k gives E multiplier (1+(−1)^k)/2, zero for odd k. A shifted even comparator remains even and has zero dot product with any odd vector. Thus its zero response cannot erase the odd measurements. S05 R017/R021 describe that source comparison limitation. Ternary 1,3,9,27 differs from the proposed cutoff ladder 1,3,5,9,15,25,41. R026 is cutoff/dimension synthesis; R027 compares 27−25=2; R028 retains a pathway, not a closed identity. Four-point bins alias modulo 4 and do not resolve native 25 or 27 uniquely.

Rubric — 36 points

Each step earns four separately observable points: one for declared inputs/units and denominator domains; one for the correct operation and calculation; one for reconstruction or correct source classification; one for retained signs/components and stated limits. Use the task-specific evidence below.

Do not penalize equivalent notation if explicitly typed. Allow honest rounding differences in step 7; require their precision and reconstruction rule. Correct arithmetic without source scope does not earn the boundary point. No point requires fresh raw-data acquisition or a claim that the source's open measurement obligations have been closed.
