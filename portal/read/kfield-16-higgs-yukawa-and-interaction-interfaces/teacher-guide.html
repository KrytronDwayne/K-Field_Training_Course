Teacher guide

This topical senior course develops a replayable interaction ledger from signed arithmetic. Use twelve core 55-minute periods and three capstone periods (825 minutes total). The seven lesson boundaries follow the approved dependency map. Extra time within lessons 1,2,4,5 is already included; do not compress the contraction and dual-rule bridge into a definition-only lecture.

Every core period allocates 5 minutes to retrieval, 20 to explanation and worked examples, 15 to practice, and 15 to independent diagnostics and repair. This gives 60 retrieval, 240 teaching, 180 practice, and 180 diagnostic minutes across the core. Within multi-period lessons use the focus named in the lesson rather than repeating the whole text. Capstone periods allocate 5 minutes to task setup, 40 to independent work, and 10 to checking/defense; this adds 165 minutes.

Entry check: ask learners to calculate −4+1+3, add (2,0)+(1,1)+(0,1), and explain why an ordered list differs from an unordered set. Answers are 0, (3,2), and that order can affect an operation such as an oriented product. If course-15 terminology is unfamiliar, use lesson 1's full sector table and basis bridge. No advanced algebra is an assumed computation skill.

Teach wedge signs with five labeled cards. A neighboring exchange flips a separate sign card. Physically remove one card for contraction while retaining its original position to determine the sign. Check down in its exact Q-first order; a correct final +1 without the four-swap reasoning is incomplete evidence of mastery. For phases, begin with i²=−1 and i(−i)=1 before writing U. General determinant calculations and Lie theory remain recognition-only.

Require learners to attempt P1/P2 before uncovering their worked answers. For independent evidence, have them submit an initial record and a corrected record with the reason for each change. Diagnostics may be discussed after initial answers. Keep mistakes and revisions as a learning record; do not erase weak channels or signed residuals. A wrong dual phase is especially valuable: it provides a clear failed test whose output differs by a sign.

The lesson answers are complete keys; the capstone contains a 100-point analytic rubric. Use its mastery guidance, but repair essential-operation errors even if other points compensate. Retesting should use changed finite components or inputs with the same taught algorithm, not a new hidden prerequisite. Suitable quick variants include swapping c2,c3 in an up product, v=6 for a neutral vacuum norm, or an identity coefficient matrix. The lesson examples already supply their algorithms.

Unit boundary after lesson 2: confirm vector versus covector, q versus electric charge, and source formula order. After lesson 5: check units, separate Y_h/Y_Yuk, nonzero domains, and coherent coordinate changes. After lesson 7: check every capstone operation against answered practice and ensure the contract leaves unspecified physical state inputs blank. These are prerequisite gates, not empirical significance tests.

Evidence discipline: no dataset was acquired for this course. S02 supplies source-local definitions and parameterized relations; exact paths and hashes are in sources.md and sources.json. Students freshly recompute finite examples. The source's action names are architecture, not measured energies. The integration's compiler authority labels differ from the mathematical backbone. Do not assign physical mass eigenvalues to arbitrary entries of the synthetic mass matrix, or infer a rate from an energy difference.

Reference theory enters through the source's familiar Higgs/Yukawa vocabulary and formulas. The course assesses their stated finite contracts, not a full derivation of quantum field theory or a claim that all physical parameters follow from structure. Proposed measurements in lesson 7 may constrain combinations only under a supplied observation model. Preserve degeneracies and unresolved inputs.

For recovery, consult manifest.json; verify its hashes before trusting passed checkpoints. The course assembly contains lesson text verbatim and adds no new teaching. verify_examples.py reruns lightweight finite checks in tf_gpu_env. Run the project validator after any repair and refresh assembly and hashes. No PDF rendering is required for this Markdown delivery.
