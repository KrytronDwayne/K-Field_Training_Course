What this course freshly verifies

All seven finite lesson examples, practice calculations and ten capstone answers use explicit inputs. verification.json records 183 checks from independently implemented matrix/vector operations, exact rational arithmetic for cubic phases, source hashes, JSON-pointer resolution and E/W/P/D/capstone IDs. independent-audit.json records a separate review of mathematical reasoning, source fidelity, prerequisites and teaching alignment. A script checking IDs is not itself a teaching audit.

The source's general positive-matrix determinant identity is read as a supplied source statement; the hand-solvable diagonal specialization is freshly recomputed. The infinite U is explicitly defined in the lesson and distinguished from finite S, rectangular T, full B_8 word space and source radial T_0. No measured signal, statistical implication, physical mechanism, phase prediction or occupation count is fabricated. Proposed mechanisms could be explored later with additional explicit premises, but are not used to fill replay gaps.

Every transformation retains the original small teaching input and enough complement/operation information to reconstruct it. Signs and zeros remain part of the ledger. Hash validation concerns recovery integrity; finite verification concerns the stated toy identity; archival status concerns the source's own claim. These are distinct evidence layers.
