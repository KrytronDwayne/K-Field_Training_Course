Reproduction

It reads original sources, verifies their hashes, checks 12,030 signed rational rounding cases against an independently written nearest-neighbor rule, and verifies all small ledger identities exactly. It retains signed residuals and finite source comparisons. It uses no tolerances, statistical erasure, packages, raw-data exports, or large arrays.
