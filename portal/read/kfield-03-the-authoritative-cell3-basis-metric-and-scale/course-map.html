Course map — The Authoritative Cell3: Basis, Metric, and Scale

Audience: high-school seniors who can use signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, linear equations, and coordinate graphs. Course-02 supplies vector and coordinate experience; Lessons 1–4 explicitly bridge three components, magnitude, dot product, transpose, matrix multiplication, cross product, determinant and inverse. No calculus, abstract algebra, quantum mechanics, or previous university linear algebra is required.

The six core lessons are 55 minutes each. Two capstone periods add 110 minutes: total 440 minutes. Unit A is Lessons 1–3 (ordered geometry); Unit B is Lessons 4–6 (inverse, scale and reproducibility). Retain the six conceptual lesson IDs while allowing extra teaching periods. Add fraction and block-inverse practice before Lesson 5 when needed, and recheck those capabilities before dependent work or independent assessment. The 440-minute total is a starting plan, not a fixed limit.

Dependency order: course-02 capabilities → lesson-01 → lesson-02 → lesson-03 → lesson-04 → lesson-05 → lesson-06 → capstone. Each lesson links its next dependency. The geometry card is source-reading support, not an untaught computational prerequisite. Full long-decimal calculations are teacher-replay support; students derive with the small analogue and interpret supplied residual components.

The approved face-area order is ab/ac/bc, while the source normalized area ray is bc/ac/ab. No lesson-boundary refinement was needed. A conditional scale theorem is taught with fixed nondegenerate angular frame and positive supplied ratios; its physical mass interpretation is deferred to course-08. Every core capstone operation is explicitly practiced in the mapped lesson.

Deliverables: six separate lessons, verbatim assembled course, geometry card, glossary, teacher guide, capstone with full answers/rubric, compact sources, verification script/record, recovery manifest and audit/completion reports. Markdown is the delivery format; no paginated rendering is required.
