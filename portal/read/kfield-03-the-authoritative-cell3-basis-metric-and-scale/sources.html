Evidence and unresolved scope

Source facts: the locked strings, conventions and reported claims. Derived mathematics: dot/cross/triple products, inverse identities and conditional positive-scale algebra. Fresh numerical witnesses: complete Decimal residual arrays in verification.json. Synthetic examples: clearly labeled integer analogue, right-angle ray, and rounding illustration. No new empirical observations, mechanisms or statistical claims are introduced. The physical origin of the angular/ratio inputs and area anchor is outside this course. The arithmetic behind the source-reported closure scalar is not specified here and has not been reconstructed. This does not affect the independently declared residual checks.

The full cross-course invariants remain in input-contract.json. Irrelevant later topics (gauge carriers, parity families, extension/compiler authority, celestial dynamics and energy interpretation) are not used as premises in this geometry course. Cell3 and galaxy-derived cells are not merged.
