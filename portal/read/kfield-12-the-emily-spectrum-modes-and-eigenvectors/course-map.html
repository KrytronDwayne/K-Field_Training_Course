The Emily Spectrum: Modes and Eigenvectors · Course map

Audience: high-school seniors with signed arithmetic, fractions, powers, linear equations, coordinate graphs, and course 11's node/edge language. Lessons 01–03 explicitly bridge matrix action, square roots, eigenpairs, dot products and normalization. No calculus, quantum mechanics, abstract algebra, or general spectral theorem is required.

The seven stable lesson IDs are retained. The approved teaching amendment expands lessons 03–06 to two 55-minute periods each, giving 11 core periods plus two capstone periods (715 minutes). Period allocation is recorded in the teacher guide. The expansion permits exact algebra and separately taught carrier distinctions; it does not change the topic kernel.

Dependency chain: lesson-01 → lesson-02 → lesson-03 → lesson-04 → lesson-05 → lesson-06 → lesson-07 → capstone. Units end after lessons 03, 05 and 07.
