Distinct evidence layers

Source facts: the identified files contain the graph, ledger, parity and decoder statements at the listed pointers. A source-reported proof or audit status is a statement in that source.

Fresh derived implications: row equations, lengths, dot products, all 27 product eigenpairs, multiplicities, reconstruction, retained mean/residual, and the exact M17 rank are checked independently in this course. They establish the stated finite mathematical contracts only.

Interpretations: “mode” is a chosen eigenvector; “observable coordinates” is used here as the source's mean-separated 13+13 coordinate count with mean retained for reconstruction. It does not identify a 26-eigenmode operator.

Empirical observations and mechanisms: no experimental measurements are introduced. All f values in exercises are synthetic. No dynamics, force, physical frequency, or energy transfer follows from adjacency or count agreement alone in this course.

Reference mathematics: finite matrix action, square roots, dot products and reversible basis expansion are taught explicitly. General spectral theorems, pseudoinverses, formal compiler adjudication and global closure remain outside assessed scope.

Unresolved external scope: S03's global formula-DAG repair and live formal integration authority are not certified. The failed graph-object retrieval is recorded above.
