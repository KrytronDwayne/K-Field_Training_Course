Capstone — A typed family-projector account

Work over three 55-minute periods. Use lessons and source cards; no external physics facts or proof certificates are required. Period 1 covers parts 1–3, period 2 covers parts 4–6, period 3 covers parts 7–9 and checking. All numerical data below are dimensionless synthetic teaching inputs. Keep ordered details, zero coefficients, complements, reconstructed variables and unresolved source branches.

Submit a single report with a typed accounting table, calculations and the source-status comparison. For every table column name what it counts: ambient dimension, independent image rank, family address, history direction or numerical coefficient. Do not use the word “states” without specifying which count is meant.

Student tasks — 100 points

  1. C20-CAP-01 (10 points). For X=F₃³ and λ(x)=x₁+x₂+x₃ modulo 3, enumerate C₀,C₁,C₂ using the nine ordered pairs from lesson 1. Explain why 9+9+9 exhausts X without overlap. Classify (2,2,2).
  2. C20-CAP-02 (10 points). On C₂ in that order take f=(9,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0). Normalize its class indicator, compute mean, full detail and normalized-indicator coefficient, and reconstruct f. Account for the 27 coordinates as uniform plus detail sectors.
  3. C20-CAP-03 (12 points). Use the declared synthetic L={(a,b,0)}, Pᵥδₓ=δₓ₊ᵥ and orthogonal lift labels. For f=2δ_(1,0,0), check V=(1/3)ΣPᵥ⊗|v⟩ by norm and reconstruction. What changes if there are 27 unitary labels with the same factor? Separately calculate Fe₁ and reverse it using F†. State the source replay boundary.
  4. C20-CAP-04 (12 points). Use lesson 4's R with first 2-by-2 block entries all 1/2 and last diagonal entry 1. Verify R²=R and its rank using column equations. Apply it to (4,2,−1) and retain the complement. List the 2-by-3 product labels and compare the dimension and rank of I₂⊗I₃⊗diag(1,0).
  5. C20-CAP-05 (12 points). Build a typed factor table for Qₙ=I₁₆⊗I₃⊗qₙ, assuming qₙ is a nonzero orthogonal rank-one projector on a finite history space of dimension H. Give the independent image basis, full dimension, total image rank and rank per family. Interpret (1,1,1) at the history-count level. Test qₙ=0 and rank(qₙ)=2. State the additional condition for different history projectors to be orthogonal.
  6. C20-CAP-06 (12 points). Compare M16, F038, X033, the D10 guard and AUD-007 in a source-status table with locators. Separate the freshly checked implication, reported occupation, guard conflict and proof-body retrieval. Explain how VERIFIED_FROZEN and VERIFIED_NOT_LOADED should be treated.
  7. C20-CAP-07 (8 points). State which physical masses, mixing values and coefficients are supplied by the label count or Weyl basis. Explain using Y=I and Y=2I. For the synthetic K=[[3,1],[2,2]], s=(7,8), give u,w and check both original equations. Show the full derivation in part 9.
  8. C20-CAP-08 (12 points). For P,D and B_ab=DᵃPᵇ as in lesson 3, take Y=2I+D−P². Write its nine entries, extract all nine y_ab by trace, and reconstruct Y. Explain the role of zero coordinates and why reversing D/P order is not a silent substitution.
  9. C20-CAP-09 (12 points). Derive S and r for part 7's system, solve and reconstruct w, verify determinant and both equation residuals. Then solve K=[[1,1],[1,0]], s=(3,1) with E=0 directly. Finally classify K=[[1,1],[1,1]] with s=(2,2) and s=(2,3). Do not divide by zero in any branch.

Full worked answer key

  1. For each pair (x₁,x₂), x₃=r−x₁−x₂ modulo 3. The ordered third-coordinate lists are C₀: (0,2,1,2,1,0,1,0,2); C₁: (1,0,2,0,2,1,2,1,0); C₂: (2,1,0,1,0,2,0,2,1). Append these entries to the corresponding ordered pairs to obtain every triple. There are nine pairs and a unique third entry for each r; a triple cannot have two different residues. Thus the union has 27 nodes and no overlap. λ(2,2,2)=6 mod 3=0.
  2. Φ₂ has value 1/3 on nine nodes and zero elsewhere, so ||Φ₂||²=9/9=1. The mean is 1, detail is (8,−1,−1,−1,−1,−1,−1,−1,−1), coefficient is 3. At the first node 3/3+8=9; at every other node 3/3−1=0. Details sum to 0. Eight detail choices per class plus three means give 3+3×8=27 independent coordinates. Retain all nine displayed detail entries even though only eight are free.
  3. In each v=(a,b,0) block, the sole nonzero entry is at (1+a mod 3,b,0), amplitude 2/3. Squared norm is 9×4/9=4. V† translates each block back and scales by 1/3, giving amplitude (1/3)×9×(2/3)=2 at (1,0,0). For 27 unitary labels the squared-norm multiplier is 27/9=3, so this input's squared norm becomes 12; a normalized coefficient would be 1/√27. Fe₁=(1,ω,ω²)/√3. The reverse outputs are (1+ω+ω²)/3=0, (1+1+1)/3=1 and (1+ω²+ω)/3=0. Exact source L and Pᵥ are not loaded; AUD-009 records that gap. The specified classroom example is not a source isometry replay.
  4. R maps (a,b,c) to ((a+b)/2,(a+b)/2,c); applying again gives the same output. Columns 1 and 2 coincide. For c₁=(1/2,1/2,0) and c₃=(0,0,1), αc₁+βc₃=0 forces α/2=β=0, so rank 2. R(4,2,−1)=(3,3,−1); complement (1,−1,0); their sum restores the input. Product labels are (a,0),(a,1),(a,2),(b,0),(b,1),(b,2). The tensor carrier has 12 slots and only the six with final label 0 survive, so rank 6. A six-label table without a map does not alone compute an image rank.
  5. The factors have dimensions 16,3,H and ranks 16,3,1. Image basis vectors are eᵢ⊗fᵣ⊗hₙ, i=1,…,16 and r=0,1,2, with hₙ spanning the history image. Independent product addresses give 48 image vectors. Full dimension is 48H; each fixed family has image rank 16 and one history direction. The history counts form (1,1,1), not measured densities. qₙ=0 gives rank 0; rank two gives 96 (H≥2). Neither alternative satisfies the original rank-one premise. Orthogonality for n≠m additionally needs qₙqₘ=0; rank one alone does not imply it.
  6. M16: S02#/backbone_sequence/15 records rank and occupation. F038: S02#/proof_nodes/78/formula reports exact_simultaneous_family_occupation at common-history scope. X033: S02#/proof_nodes/81/formula reports exact_rank_48_family_projector. D10: S02#/proof_nodes/78/shared_proof_steps/0/guard says “No physical occupation conclusion.” AUD-007: S03#/findings/6 asks for the connecting selection proof and removal of the obsolete guard if discharged. The conditional rank implication is checked here. The source occupation claim remains reported; the conflicting guard is retained; the physical-carrier selection proof body is not loaded. The two capitalized verification labels are source-local dispositions, neither fresh certificates nor a reason for automatic downgrade. A physical-state-to-image dependency path is required to resolve the recorded conflict.
  7. No numerical physical mass, mixing value or physical Y is fixed by label count or basis choice in this course. Y=I has y₀₀=1; Y=2I has y₀₀=2, with all other Weyl coefficients zero in both. They use the same labels. In the supplied scalar system u=3/2,w=5/2, giving 3u+w=7 and 2u+2w=8. These are synthetic coordinates, not mass predictions.
  8. Y has rows [3,−1,0], [0,2+ω,−1], [−1,0,2+ω²]. For b=0, trace extraction gives [2Σω^(−aj)+Σω^((1−a)j)]/3, hence y₀₀=2,y₁₀=1,y₂₀=0. For b=1 all accessed entries are zero; for b=2 the sum is −Σω^(−aj)/3, giving y₀₂=−1,y₁₂=y₂₂=0. Full table by rows a and columns b: [[2,0,−1],[1,0,0],[0,0,0]]. Reconstruction is 2I+D−P², including its six zero coefficients. Zero coefficients specify the supplied matrix as exactly as nonzero ones. Since DP=ωPD, reversing order changes phase conventions and requires changing coefficients consistently.
  9. E=2; S=3−1×2/2=2; r=7−1×8/2=3; u=r/S=3/2; w=(8−2u)/2=5/2. det K=3×2−1×2=4=ES. Residuals are 3u+w−7=0 and 2u+2w−8=0. For E=0, the full equations u+w=3,u=1 give u=1,w=2, residuals zero and determinant −1. The E-inverse route is unavailable but the full matrix is invertible. For the final matrix E=1,S=0: s=(2,2) has r=0 and solutions (t,2−t), with t free; s=(2,3) has r=−1 and no solution. Preserve the free coordinate in the first case and the incompatibility in the second.

Award method credit when an isolated arithmetic error is carried consistently. A wrong physical-promotion statement cannot be offset by arithmetic points: require repair of part 6. Likewise require repair if a student discards detail, a complement, zero coordinates, w or a singular branch. Suggested mastery is 80/100 plus those scope and reconstruction repairs; this is a classroom assessment rule, not a criterion for rejecting data. The teacher guide maps each part to prior explanation, answered practice and diagnostics.
