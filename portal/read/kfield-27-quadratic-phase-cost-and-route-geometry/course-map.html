Quadratic Phase Cost and Route Geometry — Course map

Audience: high-school seniors. Entry capabilities are signed arithmetic, fractions, powers, linear equations and coordinate graphs, with series background from courses 10, 17, 25 and 26. This course supplies local bridges for vectors, dot products, square roots, matrices, transpose, inverse of a diagonal matrix, units, finite accumulation and bound proofs. No university linear algebra or calculus is assumed. Complete sequence: 13 core periods and three capstone periods, each 55 minutes (880 minutes total).

Dependency and source invariants

Units are chosen per contract: inverse-length-squared classroom G gives τ⁻¹ path cost; the installed QPC trajectory convention yields m²/s with its dimensionless metric. Do not mix the ruler matrix from Lesson 2 with the route matrix in Lessons 1 and 3–5. The pullback uses columns and x−O_K, with fixed origin and basis. Cell3 geometry is source-locked and not replaced by our 2D positive-diagonal example. No parity, gauge carrier, recursion, galaxy scale or frame-motion construction is introduced by analogy.

Preserve every signed vector, duration, source branch and closure residual. Source reported exactness, retrieved evidence, synthetic derivation and proposed physical transfer are separately typed. Full source numeric replay is gated; the approved core source task audits precisely that gap.

Capstone closure

The concrete input→output map is in capstone.md, “Backward map.” Every required operation appears in E1/W1 and answered P1 before its capstone step. Tasks use supplied finite data. The source audit needs only the source cards reproduced locally; absent matrices are identified as absent, not requested as hidden homework. The complete key awards 28 points across seven operations.
