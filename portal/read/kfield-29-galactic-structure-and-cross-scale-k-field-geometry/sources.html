Evidence vocabulary

Source fact: a statement read in a pinned source. Derived implication: algebra or computation from declared inputs. Interpretation: an explanatory reading of a result. Mechanism: a proposed process generating it. Reference mathematics: established dot-product, inverse and metric rules taught locally. Unresolved information: missing calibration, missing source arrays, selection inputs, closure residuals or model discrepancies. Never promote a source title, fitted similarity, or graph label into a verified physical mechanism.
