Photometer Experiments and Information-Preserving Analysis

Audience: high-school seniors. Entry capabilities: signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, simple linear equations and coordinate graphs. Courses 01,07,24–27 are named background prerequisites; this course teaches its finite operators explicitly.

Eight lessons occupy 15 periods of 55 minutes (825 minutes); the capstone adds three periods (165 minutes), total 18 periods / 990 minutes. Unit A: lessons 1–3 (records and ratios). Unit B: lessons 4–6 (comparators, events and scale). Unit C: lessons 7–8 (uncertainty and prospective tests).

The original lesson order and exercise IDs are preserved. Lesson 4 includes explicit complex-number arithmetic and inverse sums to close the DFT prerequisite. Its three periods separate reflection, half-turn parity, and comparator/DFT/source branches. No other course is required to supply missing operations.

Recovery gate at each lesson: reproduce the worked input, answer diagnostics, and repair any misunderstanding before its dependent lesson. Unit reviews compare definitions, support and evidence scope. See teacher-guide.md. All nine capstone operations have a named prior practice; the capstone introduces no new transform.
