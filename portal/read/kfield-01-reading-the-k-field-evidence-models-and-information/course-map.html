Reading the K-Field: Evidence, Models, and Information — course map

Audience: high-school seniors who can use signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, linear equations, and coordinate graphs. Five 55-minute lessons plus two 55-minute capstone periods. No calculus, quantum mechanics, abstract algebra, or prior matrix algebra is required.

The approved five-lesson sequence is retained. Each adds one central capability. Coordinate triples and a diagonal return example are taught in Lesson 5; full source matrix derivations remain recognition-only. The source identity is introduced for reading in Lesson 2, then a finite analogue is computed in Lesson 5.

Dependency order: lesson-01 → lesson-02 → lesson-03 → lesson-04 → lesson-05 → capstone. Unit A (1–2) establishes evidence literacy; Unit B (3–4) establishes lossless comparison; Unit C (5 and capstone) assembles the research map. Cumulative checks follow lessons 2, 4, and 5.

Evidence scope

Use S02's exact mature identity and reported closure discrepancy, and S06's exact reported angular-response witness. Source statuses remain attributed. No raw record analysis or formal compilation is claimed. Synthetic tables are separate. Series-wide advanced invariants stay in input-contract.json as author constraints, not hidden prerequisites.

Navigation and capstone closure

Read lesson-01.md through lesson-05.md or the identical lesson assembly in course.md. terminology.md is the lookup sheet; sources.md holds full paths, fingerprints and locators. teacher-guide.md gives pacing and reteaching decisions. capstone.md contains the assignment, separated teacher answers, a 40-point rubric and an operation-by-operation prior-practice map. verification.json and verify_examples.py record and replay finite example checks.
