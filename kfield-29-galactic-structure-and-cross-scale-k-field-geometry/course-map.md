# Galactic Structure and Cross-Scale K-Field Geometry — course map

Audience: high-school seniors. Sixteen 55-minute core periods plus three capstone periods, 1,045 minutes total. This is a topical course with connected explanations and finite calculations. It assumes signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, linear equations and coordinate graphs. Earlier courses 04,06,10,22,24,26 supply context; all vector, phase, matrix, inverse, quadratic, eigen-direction and normalization operations actually assessed are taught locally.

Read [sources](sources.md) for provenance, [terminology](terminology.md) for symbol types, and [teacher guide](teacher-guide.md) for pacing and repairs. [course.md](course.md) contains the lessons verbatim in order. [capstone](capstone.md) supplies the assessment and full answers.

E1 and W1 IDs in the table have prefix C29.NN matching their lesson. CAP-NN means C29-CAP-NN. Every concept is introduced, practiced, diagnosed, assessed and used later. Source-amendment SA-29-E11 is applied across the eight lessons: direction/axis separation, historical response scope, phase versus center units, positive metric bridge, skew counterexample, full states and frozen comparison.

Dependency chain: arithmetic and graphs → L01 → L02 → L03 → L04 → L05 → L06 → L07 → L08 → capstone. Units are: I directions/response (L01–02), II reconstruction/metric (L03–05), III occupancy/prediction (L06–08). Check cumulative terminology, source roles and capstone readiness after each unit.

The approved lesson boundaries are retained. Dense lessons receive the blueprint's two or three periods; the map does not compress them into one. One mathematical repair to a supplied teaching example replaces three equal 2π/3 loop phases with the closure-consistent triple (2π/3,4π/3,2π/3). Another clarification explicitly separates full-step addresses from half-step window labels. These repairs preserve the kernel and are recorded in audit_report.json.
