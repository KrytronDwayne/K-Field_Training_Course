## Unit boundaries

After lesson-02, require reversible three-channel reconstruction and the integer-lattice counterexample. After lesson-04, require exact row decoding, source-L distinction and dimensional rendering. After lesson-06, require composition invariance, endpoint conventions and source cards. After lesson-08, require complete report reconstruction. Each capstone task maps to an earlier answered practice in capstone.md.

## Scope and assumptions

- A topical short course, not a semester per topic. Pacing is 55-minute core lessons plus capstone periods; split dense lessons if actual learner load requires it.
- No prior calculus, abstract algebra, university linear algebra, or quantum mechanics unless explicitly taught earlier.
- Required core assessments must be solvable with taught finite examples. Clearly label deeper recognition-only extensions.
- Markdown is the delivery format; no PDF or Word generation is required.
- Named earlier courses provide context; local bridges make finite core operations self-contained.
- General representation theory, physical coefficient selection and unprovided phase tensors are recognition/retrieval boundaries, not assigned computations.
- Source mathematical M17 requires M05 and M11; this course teaches the finite prerequisites needed for its explicit decoder, without treating compiler module labels as the same module system.
- No rounding half-tie, Cell3 coordinate ownership, gauge permutation or metric substitution is used in the core arithmetic. Their source conventions remain unchanged in the input contract.
