# Reading the K-Field: Evidence, Models, and Information — course map

Audience: high-school seniors who can use signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, linear equations, and coordinate graphs. Five 55-minute lessons plus two 55-minute capstone periods. No calculus, quantum mechanics, abstract algebra, or prior matrix algebra is required.

The approved five-lesson sequence is retained. Each adds one central capability. Coordinate triples and a diagonal return example are taught in Lesson 5; full source matrix derivations remain recognition-only. The source identity is introduced for reading in Lesson 2, then a finite analogue is computed in Lesson 5.
