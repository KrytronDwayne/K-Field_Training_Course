# Course map — History, Orientation, and Chirality

This topical course serves high-school seniors with signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, linear equations and coordinate graphs. Series prerequisites are courses 06, 15 and 17. Their operational bridge is explicit here: retain ordered labels and signed coordinates, distinguish carriers and preserve complements. No university linear algebra, calculus or quantum mechanics is assumed. Matrix action, products, adjoints, norms, projectors, diagonal determinants, complex arithmetic and source-reading conventions are taught locally before assessment.

The seven lesson boundaries follow the approved map. Multi-period lessons retain their stable IDs with explicit session breaks, so no operation has been dropped to fit one period. Twelve core periods plus three capstone periods total 825 minutes. Each core period budgets retrieval 5, explanation/worked example 20, practice 15 and independent diagnostics/repair 15 minutes. A teacher may add recovery time without dropping an operation.

## Units and completion boundaries

Unit A (01–04) develops recoverable history algebra; its cumulative check compares S, T and U and reconstructs every projector component. Unit B (05–06) separates geometric orientation, abstract grading and representation labels; its cumulative check compares ledgers and verifies Π. Unit C (07) integrates source reading with a distinct CP branch; its cumulative check distinguishes source report, finite verification and physical prediction.

Finite classroom examples are dimensionless. The actual Cell3 basis retains its locked units, ICRS column order and negative determinant; it is not replaced by the diagonal toy. A complete Clifford realization, radial T_0 replay, physical chirality selection, angular Hamiltonian, occupation rule and CKM/PMNS prediction are recognition/scope boundaries, not hidden capstone tasks. The source gaps remain visible in sources.md and completion_report.json.
