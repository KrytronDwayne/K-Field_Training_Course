# Course map — Recursive K-Field Geometry and Address Histories

Audience: high-school seniors with signed arithmetic, fractions, powers and coordinate reading. Six lessons occupy seven 55-minute core periods; the capstone takes two more. Course 05 capabilities are restated as operational bridges in lessons 01–03, so core tasks do not depend on an unavailable prior lesson file.

Dependency chain: lesson-01 → lesson-02 → lesson-03 → lesson-04 → lesson-05 → lesson-06 → capstone. Unit A (01–03) develops geometry and typed counts. Unit B (04–06) develops modular words and lossless histories.

## Pacing and scope decisions

Lesson03 retains its stable checkpoint but spans two periods (110 minutes), as approved. All other lessons use 55 minutes: 20 explanation/worked examples,15 practice,15 diagnostics/feedback,5 transition. Lesson03 uses that breakdown twice. Capstone periods divide steps01–04 and05–09 with review time. Infinite inverse limits and abstract quotient proofs are recognition-only; no calculus, general matrix inversion or physical modeling is assessed.

Assumptions: a topical short course; exact integer and fraction computations; Markdown delivery; no new source measurements. Source evidence and arithmetic derivations remain separate. All removed address detail remains in reversible records.
