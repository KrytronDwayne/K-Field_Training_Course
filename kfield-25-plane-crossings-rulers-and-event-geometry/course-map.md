# Plane Crossings, Rulers, and Event Geometry — Course map

Audience: high-school seniors. This topical course uses seven lessons over eleven 55-minute core periods, followed by three 55-minute capstone periods (770 minutes total). All finite computations use supplied synthetic inputs. Markdown is the delivery format.

Entry capabilities are signed arithmetic, fractions, ratios, powers, substitution in linear equations and coordinate graphs. Courses 04, 05 and 24 are the series prerequisites; this course provides local bridges for Cartesian triples, normalization, dot products, rates, radians, angular wrap and reconstruction so no inaccessible prior worksheet is necessary. Square roots are taught through √25=5; calculus is recognition-only and never assessed.

Every period allocates 5 minutes retrieval, 20 explanation/worked example, 15 practice and 15 diagnostics/repair. Multi-period lesson session topics are named in the lessons and teacher guide. These boundaries preserve the approved teaching amendment: extra periods support moving geometry, event exceptions and ledger reconstruction without adding untaught calculus.

## Unit and recovery boundaries

Unit 1 (lessons 1–3) checks units, orientation and fixed versus changing geometry. Unit 2 (lessons 4–6) checks ruler meaning, continuity, root multiplicity and event definitions. Unit 3 (lesson 7 and capstone) checks full reconstruction, polarity, gaps, shared-event dependence and source scope. Resume from the first failed prerequisite in manifest.json. Passed checkpoint hashes must be checked before reuse; assembly inserts passed lessons verbatim.

## Scope decisions

The full moving-geometry derivative is preserved as recognition-only; finite endpoint replay provides the required senior-level computation. No live celestial prediction, detector transfer, empirical source-event replay or new Cell3 numerical derivation is requested. Exact pinned source vocabulary and authority boundaries are retained in source-notes.md and sources.json. Synthetic geometry remains separately typed from the mature locked Cell3 geometry. All original invariants remain in input-contract.json, with scope applicability recorded in source-notes.md.
